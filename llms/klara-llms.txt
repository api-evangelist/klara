# Klara

> Klara is a US healthcare patient-engagement and communication platform, now part of Modernizing Medicine (ModMed). It gives medical practices two-way HIPAA-compliant secure messaging, patient texting, appointment reminders and scheduling, intake and forms, telehealth, and automated workflow tools that reduce phone volume and staff workload. Klara does not publish its own self-serve public developer API; it integrates with 50+ EHR/practice-management systems and is listed as a partner app in ModMed's synapSYS marketplace. The ONC-certified HL7 FHIR R4 / SMART-on-FHIR developer program belongs to parent ModMed's EHR products, not to the Klara messaging product itself. Klara has no llms.txt of its own; this file is generated by API Evangelist from the public Klara surface.

## Product

- [Klara / ModMed Patient Engagement](https://www.modmed.com/what-we-do/patient-engagement/): The current Klara product page — klara.com and www.klara.com now redirect every path here.
- [Patient Communication](https://www.modmed.com/what-we-do/patient-communication/): Messaging, web chat, call-to-text, voicemail transcription; states HIPAA-compliant secure patient messaging with optional patient 2FA.
- [Klara Provider Login](https://doctor.klara.com/): Web application sign-in for practice staff.
- [Klara Patient App](https://patient.klara.com/): Patient-facing web application.
- [Schedule a Demo](https://www.modmed.com/schedule-demo/): The only self-serve commercial path. No pricing is published.

## Not Klara

Three unrelated products share the name and are routinely confused with this company in search results. None of them belongs to Klara the US healthcare patient-engagement platform:

- klara-ai.com — a partner RFI-workflow API, different company.
- klara.ch / api.klara.ch — Swiss business-administration software, different company.
- Klarna (docs.klarna.com) — Swedish payments, different company.
